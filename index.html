Where we go

Mostly the Himalayan temples and treks of Uttarakhand. Altitudes are approximate and help you plan for the cold.

  • Kedarnath3,583 mShiva jyotirlinga. Road to Gaurikund, then trek or helicopter.
  • Badrinath3,133 mVishnu dham, reachable by road all the way.
  • Gangotri3,100 mSource of the Ganga, via Uttarkashi.
  • Yamunotri3,293 mStarts at Janki Chatti with a short trek to the temple.
  • Tungnath3,680 mHighest Shiva temple, with the Chandrashila summit above it.
  • Chopta2,680 mMeadows and forest base for Tungnath, known as mini Switzerland.
  • Hemkund Sahib4,329 mSikh pilgrimage by lake, trek from Govindghat and Ghangaria.
  • Madmaheshwar3,289 mSecond Kedar, a quiet trek from Ransi.
  • Kalpeshwar2,200 mFifth Kedar near Urgam, open through the year.

Also: Rishikesh, Haridwar, Auli, Mussoorie, Nainital, Himachal, Rajasthan and the rest of India on request.
